Crochet vs Artificial Flowers — Which is Better? 🌸

August 2026

Artificial flowers are everywhere, but are they really worth it? Let's break down why handmade crochet flowers are the smarter (and cuter) choice.

We've all been there — you buy a bouquet of artificial flowers and within weeks they're collecting dust and looking sad. Here's why crochet flowers are different:

🌿 Longevity — Crochet flowers don't fade, wilt, or shed. They look the same after years. Artificial flowers lose colour and petals over time.

💚 Eco-Friendly — Our flowers are made from yarn, not plastic. No microplastics, no waste. Better for the planet.

✨ Uniqueness — Every single ZYLO flower is handmade. No two are exactly alike. Try finding that in a factory-made bouquet.

🎁 Sentimental Value — A handmade gift says "I actually put thought into this." That hits different.

💰 Price — Our crochet bouquets start from Rs. 1,800 and last forever. Artificial bouquets of similar size cost Rs. 1,200-2,000 and end up in the trash.

The verdict? Crochet flowers win — by a landslide 🧶🏆

CARE GUIDE

How to Care for Your Crochet Flowers 💐

July 2026

Got your ZYLO crochet flowers? Here's how to keep them looking fresh and beautiful for years to come.

Crochet flowers are low-maintenance, but a little care goes a long way! Here are our top tips:

🧹 Dusting — Use a soft brush or hair dryer on cool/low to gently remove dust. Avoid wet cleaning unless necessary.

💧 If They Get Wet — Don't panic! Gently squeeze out water (don't wring), reshape, and let them air dry flat. They'll bounce back!

☀️ Avoid Direct Sunlight — Prolonged direct sun can fade colours over time. Keep them in a spot with indirect light for the brightest look.

📦 Storage — If storing for a season, place them in a breathable bag (not sealed plastic) to prevent moisture buildup.

🧴 Never Use Bleach — This seems obvious, but just in case — bleach will ruin your crochet flowers permanently!

Follow these tips and your ZYLO flowers will be the prettiest thing in the room for years 🌸✨
